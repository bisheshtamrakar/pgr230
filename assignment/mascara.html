Mascara

Mascara is a cosmetic product used to enhance the eyelashes. It is typically applied to the upper and lower lashes to darken, lengthen, thicken, and define them, creating a more dramatic and expressive eye look. Mascara comes in various formulations, including waterproof and regular, and is available in different colors, with black being the most common.

Types of Mascara


  • Waterproof Mascara: This type is designed to resist water and sweat, making it ideal for long-lasting wear. It is perfect for those who want their lashes to stay defined throughout the day, even in humid conditions.

  • Regular Mascara: This is the most common type of mascara, offering a natural look and easy application. It is suitable for everyday use and provides a subtle enhancement to the lashes.

  • Lengthening Mascara: This type is formulated to make lashes appear longer. It often contains fibers or special ingredients that add volume and length, creating a more dramatic effect.
